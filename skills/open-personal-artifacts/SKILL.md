---
name: open-personal-artifacts
description: Recover and organize local browser references and their related handoff panels.
---

The goal entry point is `workOnPersonalArtifactsGoal.nl`. Use it with the
installed Browser Data and collection access bounds for the current task.
It can recover a uniquely supported reference, curate bookmarks, and record
a verified reference on a panel. Its stages replan when an observed artifact
changes and leave unresolved references held.
